## 5 Insights on Netflix's Content Acquisition Strategy

1. **Shift from movies to TV.** Before 2015, about 85% of additions were Movies. By 2019, TV Show share reached 32%.
2. **2019 was the peak year** with 1,543 titles added. October is the busiest month (new season effect).
3. **Content is concentrated.** International Movies, Dramas and Comedies lead the genres. USA, India and UK are the top 3 countries, and India's share has grown fast since 2017.
4. **TV Shows are mostly limited series.** About 67% have only 1 season.
5. **Mature audiences are the main target.** TV-MA is the most common rating (3,207 titles).
